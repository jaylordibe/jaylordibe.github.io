# Surface: portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Experience (the work leads). Audience: hiring managers, recruiters and contract clients, equal weight. Action: email / LinkedIn. Proof: 10+ years, three featured projects, a seven-company career timetable (from Jay's CV), the stack breadth with current focus on Node.js/NestJS/Express. No vanity stats, no invented employers. Build path: code-led (no image generation). User delegated the direction choice and asked that it not default to dark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Jay's career as a live station departures board. Each project is a departure with a route, and the stack lines are platforms. It refuses the dev-portfolio defaults: a dark IDE window, a glowing gradient hero, rows of equal glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: a bright concourse page in cool platform-white #f3f4f1 with ink #121212 type. One real split-flap board, black tiles with warm-white letters and a hinge line, is the only dark object. The board family (small code, sector and year boards, footer) shares that dark material. Signal-yellow #ffc917 and platform-blue #0b3d91 flood whole sections and cut hard. A condensed signage display face is paired with a highway-sign grotesk. Full-width rules, round line bullets in each line's colour, and no badges, chips or glass.

STORY: the visitor learns (Selected work, Timetable, Network, Profile, Boarding) who Jay is and at what level, and that Jay covers backend, frontend, data and cloud and has modernised a bank's APIs. Then they board: email or LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a slim solid signage bar (mark, a mini board naming the current section, nav). The board hangs full width and flips into JAY LORD IBE / LEAD SOFTWARE ENGINEER / FULL STACK · 10+ YEARS (re-flowed on phones as JAY LORD / IBE / LEAD SOFTWARE / ENGINEER / 10+ YEARS). Under it sit one sentence of positioning and the primary action as a yellow "Now boarding" strip. The Selected work heading meets the fold.

FORM: candidate 4 of 7 (Solari split-flap departures board). Seed 127c2f93. Raises: total colour commitment (bebop), route diagrams that draw themselves in (zine), two faces with hierarchy by case and rule (terminal).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
